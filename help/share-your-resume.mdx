## Build it

Open **Resume** to edit on one side and see a live preview on the other. Everything saves as you type.

- **Basics** come from your profile: name, union status, height, hair, eyes, and representation.
- **Headshot:** upload a JPG or PNG up to 10 MB. Tap **Adjust** to drag the photo so your face sits right in the frame.
- **Credits** are grouped by Film, TV, Theatre, and Commercial, plus any category you add. Reorder them within a category.
- **Training** and **Special skills** finish it off.

## Share a link

Your page starts private. Tap **Publish page** to make it live at `callastudio.app/r/your-username`, then **Copy link** to share it. You can make it private again anytime.

You need a username first. Pick one in **Settings**. You can change it once, and the old link stops working when you do.

## Download a PDF

**Download PDF** gives you a US Letter PDF that matches the preview, named with your first and last name.
