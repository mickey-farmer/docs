title: "Forward casting emails"
description: "Send casting emails to your Calla Studio address and review them in the Inbox."
---

## Your forwarding address

Every account gets its own address, like `jordan@in.callastudio.app`. Find it in **Settings** with a Copy button.

Forward any casting email there and Calla Studio pulls out the details for you to check: the type of email, the role, project, casting office, and due date. Emails from Actors Access, Casting Networks, Backstage, and the reps you add in Settings work best.

Only emails you forward (or from reps you've added) are accepted.

## Set up auto-forwarding

So you never have to forward by hand, set up a filter in your email app that forwards casting emails to your address. If you use Gmail, it sends a confirmation code first. It shows up in your Inbox in Calla Studio so you can finish the setup in Gmail.

## Review your Inbox

Each forwarded email shows up in **Inbox** with what Calla Studio found. Anything missing or uncertain is highlighted so you can fill it in. Then:

- **Add to Auditions** to save it.
- **Edit first** to change details before saving.
- **Ignore** to skip it.

If an email matches an audition you already have, like a callback for the same role and project, you'll see **Move to Callback** (or the matching status) instead of a duplicate.
