## Create your account

Sign up with your email and a password (at least 8 characters), or with Google. If you use email, check your inbox for a link to confirm your address. The link works for 1 hour.

## Quick setup

After you sign up, a short setup asks for three things. You can skip any of them and come back later.

1. **Your name and basics.** Union status, height, hair, and eyes. These also show on your resume.
2. **Your first audition.** Add one you've already submitted for.
3. **Your forwarding address.** Your own email address for sending casting emails to Calla Studio. See [Forward casting emails](/help/forward-casting-emails).

## Settings

In **Settings** you can change your profile, username, time zone, and theme (System, Light, or Dark), add your reps' email addresses, and copy your forwarding address.
