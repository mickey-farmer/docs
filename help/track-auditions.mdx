## Add an audition

Every audition needs a **role** and a **project**. Everything else is optional: type, format (self-tape, in person, or live virtual), casting office, due date, source, notes, and sides. New auditions start as **Submitted**.

## The board

The board has five columns: **Submitted**, **Callback**, **Pinned**, **Booked**, and **Released**. Filter by type (TV, Film, Commercial, Voiceover, Theatre, or Other) with the chips at the top. On your phone, swipe sideways to see each column.

Tap an audition to open it. From there you can change its status, edit details, add callbacks, and attach sides.

## Callbacks

When you move an audition to Callback, Calla Studio offers to add the callback's details. An audition can have as many callbacks as you need: Callback, Chemistry read, Producer session, Network test, or Other.

## When you book it

Moving an audition to **Booked** adds it to **Bookings**, with the role, project, type, and casting office copied over. You can add shoot dates, location, contract type, and rate from the booking.
